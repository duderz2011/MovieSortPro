# Third-party metadata attribution

## The Movie Database (TMDB)

This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.

https://www.themoviedb.org

TMDB requires a prominently displayed approved logo as well as the notice above in the application's About or Credits section. This source-only community preview includes the notice, but does **not yet bundle an official TMDB logo**. Before distributing TMDB-enabled binary releases, add an approved logo asset from https://www.themoviedb.org/about/logos-attribution to the About / Credits UI, without changing its aspect ratio, colours or orientation. Do not imply endorsement. The project MIT licence does not claim ownership of provider names, logos, or metadata.
